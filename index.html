Here when you need us

Good hair days, with a little help.

Have a question before you choose? Here are a few helpful starting points. For anything else, send us a note.

Talk to our team ↗
How do I choose the right style?

Start with the length, texture, and everyday look you love. If you’re deciding between styles, tell us a little about what you’re looking for in the contact form and we’ll be glad to help.

How should I care for my hair?

Care needs depend on the hair type and style. Check the care guidance included with your product, and reach out if you need help finding the right routine.

Where can I find shipping or order information?

Order, shipping, and return details vary by product. Please contact us with your question or order information so we can point you to the right details.
